# PY Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

## Overview

PY Connect ("we", "us", "our") operates the PY Connect super-app platform, including the PY Connect consumer app, PY Connect Captain driver app, and PY Connect Partner merchant app, along with the web portal at https://pyconnect.run.place.

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Phone number (for OTP-based authentication)
- Name
- Email address (optional)
- Profile photo (optional, for drivers and partners)

### Location Information
- **Consumers**: Current location for ride booking, food delivery, and contextual recommendations. Location is only collected when you actively use the app.
- **Drivers (Captains)**: Real-time GPS location while you are marked ONLINE for dispatch matching and ride tracking. Background location is used to receive ride offers and send your location to riders during active rides. Background location is disabled when you go offline.
- **Partners**: Business address and service area. No real-time location tracking.

### KYC Documents
- **Drivers**: Driving License, Vehicle Registration (RC), Insurance, and selfie verification
- **Partners**: FSSAI license, GSTIN, PAN card, and bank account details

### Transaction Information
- Ride history and trip details
- Food order and booking history
- Payment method and transaction records (processed via Razorpay)
- Wallet balance and payout history (drivers and partners)

### Device Information
- Device token for push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
- Device model and OS version for compatibility
- IP address for security and fraud prevention

## How We Use Your Information

### For Consumers
- Process ride bookings, food orders, and reservations
- Provide real-time tracking and ETAs
- Personalize recommendations based on dietary preferences and location
- Send order updates and promotional notifications
- Process payments securely

### For Drivers
- Match you with nearby ride requests
- Send your live location to riders during active rides
- Track earnings and process payouts
- Verify identity and compliance with safety requirements
- Send ride offer notifications

### For Partners
- Display your business and menu to consumers
- Process incoming orders and bookings
- Process payouts to your bank account
- Verify business credentials and compliance
- Send order notifications

### For All Users
- Authenticate your identity via OTP
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations
- Provide customer support
- Improve our services

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:

- **Razorpay**: Payment processing (card, UPI, wallet transactions)
- **AWS (Amazon Web Services)**: Cloud infrastructure and database hosting
- **Firebase (Google)**: Push notifications and analytics
- **Government authorities**: When legally required for compliance or law enforcement

### Driver-Rider Information Sharing
- Riders see the driver's name, photo, vehicle number, and live location during an active ride
- Drivers see the rider's name, phone number, pickup location, and dropoff location for an active ride
- This information is no longer accessible after the ride is completed

## Data Security

- All KYC documents are stored in private cloud storage with restricted access
- All API communication is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
- Payment data is processed by Razorpay — we never store your card or UPI credentials
- Database access is restricted and audited
- OTP-based authentication prevents unauthorized account access

## Data Retention

- Active account data is retained while your account is active
- KYC documents are retained as required by law
- Trip and order history is retained for 2 years for dispute resolution
- You can request data deletion by contacting us at support@pyconnect.run.place

## Your Rights

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update incorrect information
- **Deletion**: Request deletion of your account and data
- **Opt-out**: Disable push notifications in app settings
- **Location control**: Drivers can go offline to stop background location tracking; consumers can deny location permission

## Background Location (Driver App Only)

The PY Connect Captain app uses background location for the following purposes:
- Receiving and responding to ride dispatch offers while the app is in the background
- Sending the driver's live location to riders during active rides for pickup navigation and safety
- Maintaining online status for the dispatch system while the device screen is off

Background location is only active when the driver has explicitly toggled themselves ONLINE. When the driver goes OFFLINE, background location tracking stops completely.

Drivers can revoke background location permission at any time through Android Settings > Permissions > Location.

## Children's Privacy

PY Connect is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via in-app notification or email.
