## Full Description (4000 chars max)
PY Connect Captain is the driver app for Pondicherry's homegrown ride-hailing and mobility platform. Join the Captain fleet and start earning by giving auto-rickshaw and bike taxi rides across Pondicherry and surrounding areas.

WHY DRIVE WITH PY CONNECT?
• 0% commission — you keep 100% of your earnings during our launch period
• Transparent earnings with real-time wallet tracking
• Instant payout requests directly to your bank account
• No surge gouging — riders pay a fair, capped price (max 1.5x)
• Built specifically for Pondicherry drivers, not a national generic app

ZERO-TOUCH ONBOARDING
• Self-register with your vehicle details and license plate
• Upload KYC documents (Driving License, RC, Insurance, Selfie) directly in the app
• Complete the 5-page interactive safety tutorial
• Sign the digital Terms & Safety Agreement
• Get approved and start driving — no office visits required

SMART DISPATCH
• Receive ride offers with pickup distance, trip distance, and estimated earnings upfront
• Accept or decline rides with a single tap
• OTP verification ensures the right rider boards your vehicle
• Real-time navigation to pickup and dropoff locations
• Concurrent top-N driver dispatch means faster match times

SAFETY FIRST
• In-app SOS button active during active rides
• Digital safety agreement and tutorial completion required before going online
• Rider and driver identity verification
• Trip recording and audit trail for every ride

EARNINGS & WALLET
• Real-time wallet balance
• Transparent trip-by-trip earnings breakdown
• Instant payout to your linked bank account
• Daily, weekly, and monthly earnings summaries

BACKGROUND LOCATION
The app uses background location to:
• Match you with nearby ride requests while the app is in the background
• Send your live location to riders during active rides for pickup navigation
• Maintain your online status for dispatch while your screen is off

Background location is only active while you are marked ONLINE and is disabled when you go offline. You can disable background location at any time by going offline.
