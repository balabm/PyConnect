## Full Description (4000 chars max)
PY Connect Partner is the business management app for merchants and venues on Pondicherry's super-app platform. Whether you run a restaurant, cafe, pizzeria, pub, scooter rental, taxi service, or luggage cloak business, the Partner app gives you everything you need to manage your operations from a single dashboard.

ZERO-TOUCH SELF-ONBOARDING
• Register your business with the /register flow — no sales calls needed
• Select your business category: Restaurant, Cafe, Pizzeria, Pub/Club, Scooter Rental, Taxi Operator, or Luggage Cloak
• Upload KYC documents (FSSAI, GSTIN, PAN) directly in the app
• Add your bank account for automated payouts
• Submit for admin approval and go live

CATEGORY-AWARE MENUS
• Restaurants & Cafes: Full menu management with categories, add-ons, and dietary tags (Veg, Non-Veg, Vegan, Halal)
• Pizzerias: Pizza builder with crust, size, and topping configurations
• Pubs & Clubs: Drinks menu with cocktail, beer, and spirits management
• Scooter Rentals: Fleet management with availability tracking and rental bookings
• Taxi Operators: Fleet and ride management
• Luggage Cloak: Bag capacity management and booking system

ORDER MANAGEMENT
• Real-time incoming orders with accept/decline
• Kitchen Display System (KDS) for food preparation workflow
• Order status updates: New → Preparing → Ready → Picked Up → Delivered
• Push notifications for new orders
• Active orders panel with live status tracking

BOOKINGS & RENTALS
• Manage incoming booking requests for stays, scooter rentals, and luggage cloak
• Approve or decline bookings with one tap
• Calendar view of upcoming bookings
• Active rental tracking with pickup/return management

PROMOTIONS & MARKETING
• Create and manage promotional campaigns
• Flash deals and time-limited offers
• Track promotion performance with analytics

ANALYTICS & INSIGHTS
• Revenue dashboard with daily, weekly, and monthly views
• Order volume and average order value
• Top-selling items and categories
• Customer ratings and feedback

PAYMENTS & PAYOUTS
• Automated payouts to your linked bank account
• Transparent transaction history
• Razorpay-secured payment processing

VENUE MANAGEMENT
• Update business profile, photos, and operating hours
• Toggle venue status (Open/Closed) in real-time
• Manage capacity and availability

The PY Connect Partner app is your complete business command center for Pondicherry's growing super-app ecosystem. Join the platform and reach thousands of tourists and locals exploring Pondicherry.
