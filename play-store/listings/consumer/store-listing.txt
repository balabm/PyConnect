## Full Description (4000 chars max)
PY Connect is Pondicherry's all-in-one super-app designed for tourists and locals alike. Discover the best of White Town, Auroville, and the entire Pondicherry region with a single app that brings together rides, food delivery, stays, transit, and curated local experiences.

RIDES & MOBILITY
• Book auto-rickshaws and bike taxis with transparent, capped dynamic pricing (max 1.5x)
• Real-time ride tracking with live driver location on the map
• OTP-verified ride starts for safety and accountability
• Contextual SOS button active only during your ride
• Road-based routing for accurate ETAs

FOOD & DINING
• Order from Pondicherry's best cafes, restaurants, and pizzerias
• Filter by dietary preferences: Veg, Non-Veg, Vegan, Halal
• Real-time order tracking and live kitchen status
• Exclusive deals and promotions from local partners

STAYS & HOSPITALITY
• Browse and book boutique stays, guesthouses, and heritage homes
• Date-based search with instant availability
• Curated for the White Town and Auroville experience

TRANSIT & MOBILITY
• Intercity and local transit options
• Luggage cloak services at key pickup points
• Scooter and bike rentals for exploring at your pace

LOCAL EXPERIENCES
• Curated experiences and activities around Pondicherry
• Safety-first booking with verified providers
• Context-aware recommendations based on your location and preferences

WHY PY CONNECT?
• Built specifically for Pondicherry — not a generic national app
• Transparent pricing with no surge gouging
• Driver-friendly economics with 0% commission model
• Local partners, local drivers, local experiences
• Secure payments powered by Razorpay
• Dietary-aware food discovery

Download PY Connect and experience Pondicherry like a local.
